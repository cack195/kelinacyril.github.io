Personal Introduction

Kelina Cyril

Doctoral Researcher & Biologist

Driven by curiosity about the microscopic architecture of the mind.

I explore how living neurons communicate, adapt, and build the foundation of our memories. Combining an early love for biological photography with advanced microscopy, I spend my days peering into the quiet, complex beauty of cellular networks.

For me, science is both rigorous biophysics and deep creative wonder—tracing how thought and memory physically emerge at the nanometer scale.

Core Focus Areas

Doctoral Investigations & Pillars

Fundamental questions bridging sub-micron actin dynamics within individual dendritic boutons to macroscopic cognitive behavior.

insights
Pillar 01

Dendritic Spine Kinetics & Structural Plasticity

Investigating the rapid remodeling of actin cytoskeletons in post-synaptic heads following theta-burst stimuli. We monitor volume expansions and spine head translocations continuously over 72-hour observation windows.

Primary Focus arrow_forward

Pillar 02

High-Resolution Two-Photon & STED Nanoscopy

Pushing spatial diffraction barriers deep inside living organotypic brain slices to resolve neurotransmitter vesicle clustering and receptor alignment across the synaptic cleft with nanometer precision.

Imaging Platform arrow_forward

Pillar 03

Cortical Ensembles & Synaptic Transmission

Recording calcium transient co-activation in Layer 2/3 pyramidal neurons during visual association tasks. We correlate cellular ensemble stability directly with synaptic spine longevity.

Pillar 04

Computational Connectomics & 3D Volume Segmentation

Designing deep convolutional neural network pipelines to reconstruct dense volumetric synaptic connectivity trees from terabyte-scale light and electron microscopy time-series.
